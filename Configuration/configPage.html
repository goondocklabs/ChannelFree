Get started: put your package on the air

One click adds Channel Free to Jellyfin’s Live TV (a tuner for the channels and a TV guide). Then open Live TV in any Jellyfin app.

Your package

Your package is every channel you get. Flip one on to put it in the TV guide. Genre (201+) and network (301+) channels are built from your library and start off.
